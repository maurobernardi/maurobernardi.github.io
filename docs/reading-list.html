Research profile and selection rules

Prioritize principled Bayesian methodology and statistical computation for complex data: variational inference, expectation propagation, MCMC, scalability, convergence and approximate inference. Favor latent-structure models, generalized linear mixed models, dynamic and state-space models, and network models, especially contributions combining theory and computation. Nonparametric Bayes, survival analysis and spatial statistics are secondary interests when connected to these themes. Give lower priority to deep learning, reinforcement learning or graph neural networks without substantial statistical methodology, routine applications, and software-only descriptions without a methodological contribution.

Topics: Bayesian computation; Latent and mixed models; Dynamic models; Networks; Related methodology

Followed authors: Andrew Gelman, David Dunson, Mike West, Matt Wand, Omiros Papaspiliopoulos, Chris Holmes, Simon Wood, Daniele Durante, Giacomo Zanella, Peter Hoff, Zoubin Ghahramani, Sudipto Banerjee, Nicolas Chopin, Christian Robert, Gareth Roberts, Ryan Tibshirani, Robert Tibshirani, Trevor Hastie, Bradley Efron, Martin Wainwright, Nick Polson, David Blei, Radford Neal, Michael Jordan, Edoardo Airoldi, Sylvia Frühwirth-Schnatter, Arnaud Doucet, Emmanuel Candès, Larry Wasserman, Peter Bühlmann, Yee Whye Teh

Shortlist: at most 20 papers scoring at least 4.0/10. Archive: 7 days from first appearance. Cross-listed records are deduplicated by arXiv identifier. Replacements are excluded by default.

Rule score: 2 points per topic (up to 6), 0.5 per keyword (up to 2), 1 per followed author (up to 2), minus 2 per lower-priority phrase; the total is clipped to [0,10]. With an API enabled, the model score replaces the topic base and penalty, with the same capped keyword and author boosts. Rule and LLM-assisted scores are labeled separately and need not be directly comparable.
