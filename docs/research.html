From methods to implementation

The software page connects methodological work with implementations and worked analyses. In particular, fdaSP accompanies the locally sparse function-on-function regression work, while fastQR implements the numerical ideas developed in the QR updating paper. The same computational theme also supports the variable-selection workflow illustrated in fastBVS.

Questions arising from these projects also inform the proposed thesis topics, where the scope is adapted to the degree programme and the student’s preparation.
