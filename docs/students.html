Students & Supervision

Supervision offers a setting in which a statistical question can develop into a sustained piece of research. The projects below span Bayesian computation, model selection, functional and quantile regression, time series and applications in economics, sport and the environment. They include work undertaken jointly with colleagues at Padova and other universities.

The list combines final theses identified in the supplied documents and university repositories with supervision records from my July 2026 CV. Thesis PDF links open the available final documents directly. When an archive restricts access to the PDF, a Repository record link provides the public thesis information instead. Dates for these theses refer to the thesis year or academic year. Other entries retain the topic and year recorded in the CV, without implying a completion date or current enrolment status.

Students interested in a new project can consult the thesis opportunities, which describe possible questions at Bachelor’s, Master’s and doctoral level, or contact me to discuss their interests and preparation.
