Mauro Bernardi

Associate Professor · University of Padova

Research interests

My research concerns statistical methodology and computation, with particular interests in model selection and high-dimensional statistics, quantile regression, functional data analysis and variational inference. These themes connect the development of flexible statistical models with algorithms that make estimation and uncertainty assessment practical.

The research page provides a selection of publications and a link to the University of Padova research catalogue. Software and worked examples are collected on the software page.

About

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Statistical Sciences at the University of Padova. My teaching includes statistical methods for hierarchical data, statistics for economics and management, and Statistical Models in the PhD programme in Statistical Sciences.

Teaching and resources

Course information is available on the teaching page. The software guides bring together methodological background, reproducible R examples and interpretation of the results, including functional regression, Bayesian variable selection and quantile regression.

Reading list

The selected readings bring together foundational articles and books, with notes on how to approach them. The separate arXiv reading list collects papers in statistical methodology, computation and applications, ranked against a configurable research-interest profile. The page identifies the ranking method and the date of its latest successful update.

Interested in a thesis?

Explore possible thesis topics in model selection, quantile regression, functional data analysis and Bayesian computation. Projects can be discussed at Bachelor’s, Master’s and PhD level, with scope and preparation agreed individually.
