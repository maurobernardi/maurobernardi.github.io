Teaching

My teaching at the University of Padova spans undergraduate and postgraduate programmes, including the PhD programme in Statistical Sciences. The courses listed below bring together statistical modelling and its application, with official descriptions and academic-year information available through the University teaching catalogue.

Statistical Models

Course in the PhD programme in Statistical Sciences, University of Padova.

This doctoral course examines statistical models for dependent, structured and high-dimensional data, bringing together their formulation, inferential foundations and computational treatment. Topics include multivariate time series and state-space models, filtering, hidden Markov and semi-Markov models, network analysis and graphical models, and generalised linear mixed models. Spatial and spatio-temporal modelling introduces Gaussian processes and Gaussian Markov random fields, while functional data analysis extends the discussion to observations represented by curves. Computational topics include convex optimisation, duality and majorisation–minimisation algorithms, with applications using R and Stan. The programme connects these areas through the challenges of representing dependence and carrying out inference in complex models.

STATISTICAL METHODS FOR THE ANALYSIS OF HIERARCHICAL DATA

This course develops a unified approach to modelling correlated data through mixed-effects and hierarchical models. It covers repeated measures and longitudinal data, spatial and temporal dependence, and models with coefficients that vary over time or space. Spline-based regression, generalised additive models and Gaussian Markov random fields extend the framework to nonlinear and structured effects. Likelihood-based and Bayesian inference are studied alongside implementation in R, with emphasis on model construction, estimation, validation and the interpretation of results. Lectures, case studies and programming laboratories prepare students to carry out an independent methodological or applied project.

STATISTICS FOR THE ECONOMICS AND MANAGEMENT

This introductory course develops the statistical foundations needed to analyse economic and business data. It begins with the description of individual variables and the study of association, before introducing probability, random variables and probability distributions. These concepts provide the basis for point and interval estimation, hypothesis testing, and linear regression with inference on its parameters. Lectures and practical exercises emphasise the choice of appropriate methods, the interpretation of statistical software output, and the assessment of results in their economic or business context.
